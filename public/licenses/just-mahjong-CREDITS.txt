The tile mesh alone is adopted. Unrelated third-party objects, game code,
textures and other assets from the upstream project are not bundled.

Modifications: normalized dimensions and axis convention; triangulated
polygons; clipped surface triangles at the ivory/amber material seam;
retained authored beveled shape and per-corner normals. The tile face art
comes from the Mahjong Toufu assets separately credited in the notices.
Conversion script: scripts/convert-tile-model.py
